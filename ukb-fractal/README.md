# 2) Sampling fidelity: what it **is** and how to **score** it

**Definition.** Fidelity = how well your measurement process preserves the *information* in the underlying biological trajectory you care about.

**We separate biology (latent truths) from measurements (noisy, irregular):**

$$
\underbrace{z_{k,it}}_{\text{latent (true) SBP/uACR/eGFR}}\quad\leadsto\quad
\underbrace{y_{k,it}}_{\text{what you saw}} = z_{k,it} + \epsilon_{k,it},\quad \epsilon_{k,it}\sim \mathcal{N}(0,\sigma_{k}^2).
$$

Let $k\in\{\text{SBP},\text{uACR},\text{eGFR}\}$. Latent dynamics (choose AR(1) or spline):

$$
z_{k,i,t} = \phi_k z_{k,i,t-1} + \nu_{k,i,t},\quad \nu_{k,i,t}\sim \mathcal{N}(0,\tau_k^2).
$$

**Fidelity metrics (operational):**

1. **Cadence coverage**: proportion of planned visits actually observed; median gap; 95th-percentile gap.
2. **Nyquist adequacy**: for a target frequency band (e.g., quarterly drift in eGFR), is sampling $>2\times$ the band? (binary + margin).
3. **Signal-to-noise (SNR)**: $\text{SNR}_k = \tau_k^2/\sigma_k^2$ from the state-space fit.
4. **Information gain**: reduction in posterior var of $\eta$ when adding vitals/biomarkers:
   $\Delta I = \operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{V}_\beta^{\text{base}}) - \operatorname{tr}(\mathbf{V}_\beta^{\text{base+bio}})$.
5. **Residual entropy**: spectral entropy of $(y_k - \hat z_k)$ to detect aliasing/missed structure.
6. **Missingness-as-signal**: association of visit gaps or out-of-window labs with outcomes (pattern-mixture or joint model coefficient, Wald $p$).
7. **Design effect**: inflation due to clustering/irregularity: $D = 1 + (m-1)\rho$ (guides effective N).
8. **Calibration under censoring**: E-statistic / integrated calibration index for survival predictions at fixed horizons.

Score these 0–1 and report a **Fidelity Index** (weighted composite) per site and subgroup.
